The artist

Following the lines inward.

I am Zack Covell. These works began as a practice of attention: a line meets a circle, a ratio turns into a rhythm, and suddenly the pattern is looking back at you. Sacred geometry, music, myth and the natural world are different doors into the same room.

Some pieces are precise and diagrammatic. Others open into color, symbol and dream. All of them are digital - drawn, built and tuned by hand between 2011 and 2015 - and all of them are still asking the same question: what resonates?

Many works are available as high-quality prints, priced from $5 to $50. My wife, Maria Virginia - "SilverLOVECat" - paints original canvases alongside this digital practice.
